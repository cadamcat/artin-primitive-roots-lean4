## Mathematical sources

The formalized result is from OpenAI, [*Primitive roots for every admissible integer base*](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Primitive-roots-for-every-admissible-integer-base-October-4-2026/primitive-roots-all-integer-bases.pdf). Its marked prime estimate is Theorem 3.1 of OpenAI's [*The Poisson–Dirichlet law for prime predecessors*](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/The-Poisson-Dirichlet-Law-for-Prime-Predecessors-September-24-2026/paper.pdf). The theta-function construction follows D. A. Kazhdan and S. J. Patterson, *Metaplectic forms* (Publ. Math. IHÉS 59, 1984), with the normalization the paper's appendix verifies.

The definition of `S` in [Challenge.lean](Challenge.lean) is copied from Formal Conjectures (`FormalConjectures/Wikipedia/ArtinPrimitiveRootsConjecture.lean`, commit `9d259649abe0b02d7a25f7589b872db679b35e21`, Apache-2.0).
